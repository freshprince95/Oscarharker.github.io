About

Relationships first. Requisitions second.

I've sourced and hired Backend, Fullstack, Gameplay, Graphics and Rendering engineers for two of the biggest names in tech — landing top-performer recognition at both. But the stat I'm proudest of isn't a hire count.

It's the number of times I've walked into a Talent Acquisition team mid-breakdown with a stakeholder group, and walked back out with a rebuilt relationship, an agreed hiring plan, and a hiring manager who trusts the process again.

Sourcing gets people to the table. Stakeholder management and candidate experience are what actually get them hired — and keep them recommending the process to their network afterwards.

What I do

Sourcing & headhunting

Creative, multi-channel sourcing beyond LinkedIn — GitHub, ArtStation, X-ray search, HireEZ, Entelo — built for roles everyone else calls unfillable.

Full-cycle recruitment

Own it end to end: intake, sourcing, screening, interview prep, offer and handoff — no candidate or hiring manager left guessing.

Stakeholder management

Weekly intake syncs, market data, and hands-on interview design coaching — repeatedly used to rebuild strained TA/hiring-team relationships.

Candidate experience

Prep calls, fast feedback, honest updates — the process candidates remember and recommend, offer or not.
